# 7d q50 early stopping: root cause found, recency-weight fix rejected (2026-07-29)

## The symptom

After the GOSS→bagging fix, the 2026-07-29 retrain left 7d q50 with wildly
uneven ensemble members:

| Horizon | q10 | q50 | q90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3d | 226 / 176 / 174 | 428 / 360 / 349 | 330 / 305 / 281 |
| 7d | 206 / 286 / 237 | **41 / 11 / 89** | 85 / 188 / 270 |

Members differ only by seed and `feature_fraction` (0.6 / 0.7 / 0.8), so an
8× spread in saved trees is not a parameter effect. The whole 7d q50 ensemble
trained in **3 seconds**.

## Root cause

**The 7d q50 validation curve is nearly flat, so `early_stopping(50)` trips on
noise and the stopping round is effectively random.**

Reproduced exactly on the real production frame (`build_training_data(
days_back=1460, backfilled_only=True)` → 141 items / 114,851 rows / 48
features; 110,234 train / 2,852 val, matching the retrain log):

| Frame | Train rows | Items | best_iter across seeds | Val curve improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200-item, full history | 876,404 | 200 | 284 / 229 / 328 | 5.011 → 4.697 = **6.3%** |
| **Production** | 110,234 | **141** | **32 / 16 / 94** | 5.507 → 5.491 = **0.28%** |

0.28% of achievable improvement over 1000 rounds means the stopping round is
decided by noise: **sd 41 on a mean of 47**, reproducing the shipped 41/11/89.

Why the curve is flat — a regime mismatch between the training window and the
validation window:

| Period | n | std | p10 | p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Train (all) | 110,234 | 21.9 | −9.09 | +9.96 |
| Train (last 180d) | 15,992 | **35.1** | −14.43 | +20.14 |
| **Validation** (most recent 30d) | 2,852 | 22.2 | **−18.82** | **+20.00** |

The val window carries ~2× the spread of the training set overall, and only
**14.5%** of training rows fall in the last 180 days. The model is fit mostly on
a calmer historical regime and early-stopped against the current volatile one.

`_compute_sample_weights` weighted by item volatility and direction only — no
recency term, so a 2022 row counted as much as a 2026 one.

### Ruled out

- **Target leakage.** The final train/val split has **no purge gap**
  (`train = date < split_date`, `val = date >= split_date`), which *is* a real
  inconsistency with CV — `_compute_cv_splits(purge_days=horizon)` exists and
  both CV and the A/B harnesses use it. But only 686 rows (**0.62%** of train)
  leak, and adding a 7-day purge made stopping *earlier* (mean 17 vs 47) with
  pinball unchanged. Not the mechanism.
- **Insufficient data.** 400K rows / 540 items made it **worse** (66 / 185 / 1)
  — more rows mostly add more old-regime data.
- **The GOSS→bagging fix.** 3d q50 is healthy on the same code path.

### Tree count is a red herring

3d q50's 349–428 trees are not health — 3d's tuned `learning_rate` is **0.01**
vs 7d's **0.068** (6.8×). 3d walks slowly down an equally shallow slope. Both
independent Optuna searches picked ~0.068 for 7d (0.0694 on 07-27, 0.0676 on
07-29), so the high rate is a stable preference, not a 10-trial fluke.

## The fix that didn't work

Time-decayed sample weights (`SAMPLE_WEIGHT_HALFLIFE_DAYS`, half-life 365d)
target the mismatch directly at zero training cost. A/B'd with
`scripts/ab_test_recency_weights.py` — 8 purge-gap folds on the real production
frame, all 3 quantiles, decay vs flat, same pre-registered gate as the q50 A/B
(≥0.5% relative q50 pinball, ≥half the folds, DA no worse than −0.5pp):

| Horizon | q50 pinball | Folds won | DA | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3d | −0.01% | 5/8 | −0.12pp | KEEP flat |
| 7d | −0.63% | 2/8 | −0.37pp | KEEP flat |
| 14d | −0.22% | 5/8 | +0.06pp | KEEP flat |
| 30d | **+1.23%** | 6/8 | **+1.17pp** | SHIP decay |

**Shipped disabled (`SAMPLE_WEIGHT_HALFLIFE_DAYS = 0.0`).** Only 30d passed, and
14d — also DART, also long-horizon — moved the other way, so the 30d win reads
as noise rather than mechanism. It also failed at its actual purpose: the 7d q50
stopping-round sd rose from 81 to 102. **Recency weighting does not stabilise
early stopping.**

The knob and the harness are kept (the weight multiplier costs nothing); a
30d-only ship would need confirmation with the full 3-member ensemble first.
`TestRecencyWeighting::test_production_default_is_disabled` pins the decision so
it cannot be undone by a constant edit.

## Still open

1. **Early stopping on purged CV folds instead of the single 30-day window.**
   This addresses the *measurement*, which is the actual defect. The CV block
   already fits 9 folds × 3 quantiles per horizon with `early_stopping(20)` and
   discards every `best_iteration` — but it runs *after* the ensembles, so
   harvesting those rounds needs the block reordered. Est. +1 min on a 12m30s
   retrain, plus raising CV's `num_boost_round=200` cap so the round is not
   censored.
2. **CV's 200-round cap understates the shipped model.** Production 3d q50
   trains to 349–428 rounds; CV evaluates it capped at 200. The
   `quantile-sign` CV figures are measured on under-trained models.
3. **Cap the Optuna `learning_rate` upper bound for GBDT q50** — would reduce
   stopping variance directly. Untested.
4. **No purge gap at the final train/val split.** Immaterial here (0.62%) but
   inconsistent with CV, and worth fixing on principle with an A/B.

## Caveat on the q50 A/B

`ab_test_q50_sampling.py` ran on 200-item full-history frames — 876K rows, val
ending 2025-11-24. Production trains on 110K rows from 141 items against a 2026
val window. The +5.21% / +1.76% gains were measured in a data regime production
never sees, so the effect size is less established than
`2026-07-29-q50-row-sampling.md` implies. The direction is confirmed (3d q50
went 1/22/5 → 428/360/349 in the real retrain); the magnitude is not.
`ab_test_recency_weights.py` deliberately uses the production frame instead.
